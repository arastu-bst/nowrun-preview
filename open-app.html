What changed on the first card

The content sits together

Icon, name, category, description and the button are one centred group. Before, the description stretched to fill the card and left the button stranded at the floor, reading as a separate decision.

The icon comes with them

They tapped a card with art on it. Dropping it here breaks the thread. Same art the shelf already has.

The button names the app

“Open app” could be anything. “Open Amazon Shopping” continues their choice instead of asking a second time.

The description finishes

The shelf cuts it off mid-word. There is room for all of it here, and it is the last thing worth reading before committing.

The hand-over

One surface, not two screens

The card does not swap out. The gate fades back, the launch fades in, the strip never moves. From the person’s side the thing they tapped is the thing that starts.

The launch follows the chat

Drawn here in whichever theme the chat is using, so the hand-over is not a flash from dark to light. That is a decision to confirm: today the plugin is dark and the launch screen is light.

The grid arrives with it

The gate has the warm glow the build already uses. The grid fades in only for the launch, so the two moments are not the same picture.

The strip does its job and leaves

Ready or warming is worth knowing while you are deciding; it justifies the tap. Once the steps are running they say the same thing in better words, so the strip goes and the launch takes the whole card. If the device was already warm, the steps are mostly formality and it is over in a breath.
