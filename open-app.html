The screen between the shelf and the app. A browser will not start a stream without a gesture, so this tap has to exist. It can do more than confirm: the device warms while the person reads, and what they read is what they will be able to ask for.

What changed, and why

The icon comes with them

They tapped a card with an icon on it. Dropping it here breaks the thread. It is the same art the shelf already has.

The button names the app

“Open app” could be anything. “Open Amazon Shopping” is the thing they chose, and it reads as a continuation rather than a second question.

The middle carries what you can ask

Three phrasings, drawn from the functions the app exposes. Tapping one opens the app carrying that intent, so the gesture the browser demands becomes the goal being said out loud. Apps with no tools show the button alone.

The strip tells the truth

Ready, or warming. It is already real in the build; it only needed to be worth reading. If the device is warm before the tap, the screen after this one is short.

Where the prompts come from

Not written per screen. They are the app’s own exposed functions, phrased as a person would say them. Amazon’s seven mocked tools give three good ones without inventing anything.

    Open questions

    Can the tap carry the intent?

    Opening with a chosen prompt means the plugin passes it as the first call. That is the SDK path, and it works today only for apps that expose tools.

    Does the theme follow the chat?

    Both are drawn here. The plugin surface is dark in the current build; the launch screen after it is light. One of the two should move.

    Could this be one tap instead of two?

    If the gesture on the shelf card counted, this screen would not need to exist. Worth asking whether the Play tap can carry it.
