The app is not there yet

nowrun carries no apps. When someone asks for one that is not in their nowrun, the plugin has to say so, ask, go find it, and install it, in front of them. Type in the window below and it behaves the way the real one would: your nowrun starts empty, so the first ask installs, the second one opens, and asking again in the same chat attaches to a session that is already warm.

Why the search is on screen

The find step is the whole argument. It is the only part of the flow that shows, rather than states, that nowrun had nothing to begin with.

The card asks, it does not proceed

Nothing is fetched until the button is pressed. The card states where things stand, says what nowrun is, and waits. Dismissing is the window control, not a second button competing with the one that matters.

The search is a step, not a screen

Four steps: searching, found, downloading, installing. The app sits at the top throughout, so nothing jumps between the card that asked and the card that is working. Under its name, the query the model passed to search_apps gives way to the name of the company that published what came back.

Found is the moment

It is the only beat that earns a flourish, because it is the one that proves nothing was bundled. The icon lifts, a ring goes out from it, a tick lands, and the query turns into a named publisher. Everything else on the card stays still.

No source is named

No store name appears anywhere in the flow, in either direction. The status line reads finding, not where from.

The model checks first

Above the card, the tool row shows the catalogue being checked and coming back empty. The absence is established before anything is offered.

Said once, on the first install

One line under the state line: it installs into your nowrun, not onto your phone. It answers what the person is actually weighing up, and it explains nowrun by saying what it is instead of your phone. The diagram does the rest a few seconds later, so the card does not have to.

Installing is not launching

The install run ends at Installed. Ready to use. with its own Open button, per the sheet. Two gestures, not one: agreeing to put an app in your nowrun is a different decision from running it.

The words

Used

Search and install on the button, per the sheet. your nowrun for the set of apps this person has. session for the thing that runs. streams for how it reaches the chat. find and install, in that order, because finding is the part that has to be visible.

Not used

Any store name. Any word for the machine it runs on. Any mention of what the app is packaged as. None of these appear in the interface, and none are needed to explain it.

The one real string

The line under the app name resolves to its publisher, which is what makes it read as something found rather than something bundled. Just the company: version and size are noise at this size, and nothing there names where it came from.

What is faithful, and what is not

Faithful

The window, the tool row, the card and its controls, the step line, the strip with a running clock once the app is up, and the assistant's confirmation underneath. All of that exists in the build.

Stand-in

The live screen, and the model. Typing here only drives the launch part: ask for something to buy or something to wear and it runs. Nothing else is answered, and nothing about the app's own interface is being proposed.

Timing

The step timings are a control, not a measurement. The relative lengths of the three runs are the claim: install is long, open is shorter, warm is short.

The diagram says one thing

Three beats: the app opens in the chat, ChatGPT can use it the same way you would, so you just ask. nowrun is set in the wordmark rather than an icon, because it is the thing connecting the two, not a third app.

Still open

The opening diagram only has room on the install run. On the two shorter ones there is no time for it and the carousel runs alone. That may be the right answer anyway.
