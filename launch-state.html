How it behaves

The upper half carries the idea

Either the diagram, minimal, with the two directions animating between the phones, or six slides that change every four seconds. Both stop moving at Ready.

The lower half stays quiet

Tile, name, a two-pixel bar in five segments, and one small mono line naming the step with the app’s name in it. Enough to be honest, not enough to pull the eye.

The grid breathes

The home page’s hairline grid, with its intersections rising and falling on a slow wave, and a ripple from the app tile while it loads. Low contrast on purpose. Still, under reduced motion.

Honest when there is a reason to be

A queue position replaces the step line and holds the first segment until it clears. Steps two to four run on a timer until the player has real events; one and five are real today.
