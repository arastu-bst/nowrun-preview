How it behaves

Rules, so the screen stays honest and quiet.

Five stages, one bar, always visible

Requested, Fetching, Verifying, Installing, Ready. The bar is fixed and the labels are always on screen, so nothing has to be noticed. Ready is when the app takes over.

One line at a time

There is one place for words about nowrun, and one picture with it. They change together, about every two stages. Nothing else on the screen moves.

Walk the pool, don’t shuffle

The device remembers where it is in the list, so a repeat visitor sees a new line each time. The three groups interleave, so no one load is all pitch.

Honest when there is something to be honest about

A queue position replaces the caption under the bar and holds the first stage until it clears. Everything else advances on a beat until the player has real events to drive it.

What each one shows, and what the player actually knows today. Three of the five have real signals now; the other two advance on a timer until they do.
