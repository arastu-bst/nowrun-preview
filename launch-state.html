Launching, with something to say

The first-time launch, phone first. The upper half is a quiet carousel that says what this is, one idea at a time. The lower half is the app arriving, kept quiet so the eye stays up. The grid from the home page drifts behind it, barely. Press Next load to run it again.

How it behaves

The carousel is the tutorial

Six slides, one idea each, a headline and a line. They change every four seconds on their own and stop at Ready.

The lower half stays quiet

Tile, name, a two-pixel bar in five segments, and one small mono line naming the step with the app’s name in it. Enough to be honest, not enough to pull the eye.

The grid drifts

The home page’s hairline grid, nothing else: it slides one cell diagonally over about a minute, and a soft light crosses it. You should not be able to say what moved. Still, under reduced motion.

Honest when there is a reason to be

A queue position replaces the step line and holds the first segment until it clears. Steps two to four run on a timer until the player has real events; one and five are real today.
